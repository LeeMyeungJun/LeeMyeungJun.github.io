PERSONAL VIDEO WORKSPACE

Auto Youtube

Local tools for preparing videos and uploading them to the owner's YouTube channels.

What the tools do

Auto YouTube prepares narrated videos, thumbnails and descriptions. Shorts Maker lets the owner edit selected video clips, subtitles and opening narration. Both desktop tools use Google's official authorization flow to upload completed videos privately to the channel selected by the owner.

You stay in control

The applications run on the owner's computer. This public website is an information page, not a login service or a video editor. It does not receive Google credentials, video files or channel analytics.

Auto YouTube requests video-upload, channel-read and read-only YouTube Analytics permissions. Shorts Maker requests video-upload permission. Each tool stores its own authorization locally. The owner controls uploads and any later publication in YouTube Studio.
