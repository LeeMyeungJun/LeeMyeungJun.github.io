AUTO YOUTUBE

Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Applies to the owner's personal Auto YouTube and Shorts Maker tools.

Data and purpose

The tools process locally selected video files, scripts, subtitles, thumbnails and publishing metadata to prepare and upload videos. Auto YouTube can read the authorized channel's identity, video metadata and YouTube Analytics reports to display performance summaries. Shorts Maker uses upload access for videos and thumbnails.

Google authorization and storage

Google handles sign-in and consent. The tools do not ask for or store your Google password. OAuth access and refresh tokens are saved in separate local files for each tool, and are sent only to Google's authorization and API services as needed. Local project files and cached channel reports remain on the computer unless the owner explicitly uploads or shares them. An optional remote-access feature can make the local editor available through an authenticated tunnel; keep its access link private.

Sharing and AI services

Uploads send the selected video, thumbnail and metadata to YouTube. The owner may configure external AI services for production features, which can receive the source material submitted to those features under their own policies. OAuth tokens are not production prompts. Google account authorization data and channel analytics are not sold, used for advertising or used to develop or train generalized AI models. This static information website does not collect those files or credentials.

Auto Youtube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls local retention and can delete project files, cached reports and authorization files on the computer. To stop future API access, revoke the app's access in Google Account connections and remove its local token files. Revocation can affect tools sharing the same Google Cloud app. Deleting local files does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; manage those separately in YouTube Studio.

This website

This site has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the public pages and may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
